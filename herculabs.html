The Idea

We wanted to bridge the gap between fans and athletes, making post-game engagement more meaningful.

I worked on HercuLabs, a project that reimagines fan-athlete engagement. Our solution? The Olympic Athlete Experience Simulator Booth—a VR-powered experience where fans step into the shoes of an Olympian. Instead of just watching, they feel the intensity of the competition.

So why do fans lose interest after the Olympics?

This is where we dug the internet and found that...

  • 🫥

    Remote fan support feels invisible to athletes.

  • 🏋️

    Social media doesn’t provide meaningful interactions between fans and athletes.

  • 🔍

    Fans focus on specific athletes, not the whole national team.

The biggest issue? Once the games end, the hype dies. Fans want to stay engaged, but existing platforms aren’t built for that.
