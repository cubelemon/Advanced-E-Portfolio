  • Introduction

    What if you could step into the shoes of an Olympic champion?


    Imagine the roar of the crowd, the pressure of a game-defining moment, and the exhilaration of triumph. HercuLabs brought this vision to life through the Athlete Experience Simulator Booth, a VR-powered innovation designed to create meaningful connections between fans and Olympians, extending the magic of the games far beyond the closing ceremony.

  • The Problem

    The Olympic Games are a global spectacle that captures the world’s attention, but their impact is often fleeting.


    Fans lose their connection to the athletes once the event ends, while athletes face challenges staying visible until the next competition. HercuLabs aimed to address this gap, ensuring the Olympic spirit lives on and continues to inspire .

  • The Vision

    HercuLabs envisioned a fan experience that went beyond spectating.


    By allowing users to embody their favorite athletes, the simulator created an intimate, empathetic, and long-lasting bond between fans and Olympians.

  • The Solution

    The Athlete Experience Simulator Booth transformed fan engagement through:


    1. Immersive VR Gameplay: Users could compete as Olympic legends like Ma Long, experiencing the thrill of high-stakes matches.
    2. Interactive Photo Booths: Fans captured memories with virtual avatars of their favorite athletes in iconic Olympic settings.
    3. Personalized Experiences: Fitness levels and gameplay challenges were adjustable, making the booth accessible to all participants.

  • Omnichannel Integration

    HercuLabs enhanced the booth’s impact by integrating it into a broader omnichannel ecosystem, ensuring fans remained connected across platforms:


    • A companion app delivered digital mementos, such as VR highlights and photos, directly to users’ devices.
    • The app provided exclusive athlete content, updates on future events, and an online community for fans to share their experiences.
    • This seamless connection kept fans engaged long after their physical visit to the booth.

  • The Impact

    Feedback demonstrated the transformative potential of the HercuLabs solution:


    • Deeper Connections: Fans felt a renewed appreciation for the dedication and skill of their favourite athletes.
    • Social Media Buzz: Digital keepsakes, such as photos and VR clips, drove social sharing and excitement.
    • Sustained Engagement: Fans remained active participants in the Olympic narrative, staying connected through the app.
