  • Introduction

    What if you could step into the shoes of an Olympic champion? Imagine the roar of the crowd, the pressure of a game-defining moment, and the exhilaration of triumph. HercuLabs brought this vision to life through the Athlete Experience Simulator Booth, a VR-powered innovation designed to create meaningful connections between fans and Olympians, extending the magic of the games far beyond the closing ceremony.
