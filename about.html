About Me

I've always been driven by curiosity and a desire to grow. In 2022, fresh out of high school and navigating Sydney’s rental crisis, I took a “half-gap year” (lmao) to explore different industries through part-time jobs—my side quests. From selling car accessories to teaching, photography, coffee-making, and event work, I discovered my love for understanding people’s needs and finding solutions.


With a science background and a love for design, I found my perfect match in Interaction Design. Choosing it for uni was a no-brainer, but I craved a stronger focus on front-end development. To bridge the gap, I took the initiative to enroll in Frontend Simplified and expand my skills.


Fast forward to today—I’ve interned at a digital solutions company, designing interfaces for six international projects. Now in my final semester of second year, I see my diverse background across industries and cultures as a strength. As a Malaysian, cultural nuance has always been my superpower, but working with international clients took it to the next level.


This mix of experiences helps me design holistically—understanding user needs while creating practical, development-ready solutions.


Outside of design, I’m always up for a hike (ask me about the peaks I’ve climbed!) and love making vlogs and scrapbooks to capture memories creatively.
